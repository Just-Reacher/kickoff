What You Get

Everything Your
League Needs

One system to run your entire eFootball competition from first kickoff to the final whistle.

Auto Fixture Generation
Set your teams and the system builds a full match schedule automatically — single or double round-robin, any format.
Live Standings Table
Points, wins, draws, losses, goals for and against — all updated the moment a result is submitted.
Knockout Brackets
Top teams auto-qualify for playoffs. Quarters, semis and the final are generated and tracked automatically.
My Team Dashboard
Every player sees their own fixtures, results, stats and qualification status — private to their account.
Result Verification
Players submit scores, the league owner approves. Clean, dispute-free result confirmation every time.
Fair Owner Rules
The league owner competes on the pitch like every other player. Admin power never touches gameplay fairness.
