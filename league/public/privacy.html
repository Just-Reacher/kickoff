Your privacy matters to us. We collect only what we need to run the platform and we never sell your personal data to anyone. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can control it.
Section 01

Introduction

KickOff ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share data when you use our platform at kickoff.gg and any related services.

By using KickOff, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

This policy applies to all users — whether you are creating an account, running a league, participating as a player, or simply visiting the site.

Section 02

Data We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you use KickOff:

Data Type What We Collect Why
Account Info First name, last name, username, email address, hashed password To create and manage your account
League Data League names, team names, fixture schedules, match results, standings To operate the league management features
Usage Data Pages visited, actions taken, timestamps, browser type, IP address To improve platform performance and security
Device Info Device type, operating system, screen resolution To optimise the interface for your device
Communications Messages sent to our support team To respond to your enquiries

We do not collect payment information, location data, or sensitive personal data such as health, religion, or political views.

Section 03

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account and profile
  • Operate league management features including fixtures, standings and results
  • Send essential service communications such as account verification emails
  • Ensure the security and integrity of the platform
  • Investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service
  • Improve and develop new features based on how users interact with the platform
  • Respond to support requests and customer service enquiries

We do not use your data for advertising purposes and we do not build advertising profiles based on your activity.

No ads, ever. KickOff does not show advertisements, does not partner with advertisers, and does not sell your data to marketing companies.
Section 04

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other league participants: Your username and team name are visible to other players in leagues you join. This is necessary for the platform to function.
  • With service providers: We work with trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) who process data on our behalf under strict agreements.
  • For legal compliance: We may disclose data where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the public.
  • In a business transfer: If KickOff is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, with continued protection under this policy.

All third-party service providers are contractually required to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

Section 05

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Access controls limiting who within our team can access user data
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • JWT-based authentication with appropriate expiry periods

While we take security seriously, no system is completely immune to breaches. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

Your password is never readable. We store only a one-way bcrypt hash of your password. Neither our team nor any system can read your actual password.
Section 06

Cookies & Local Storage

KickOff uses browser storage technologies to keep you logged in and improve your experience:

  • Authentication tokens: Stored in localStorage or sessionStorage to keep you logged in across sessions
  • Session preferences: We may store UI preferences such as your selected league to avoid you having to re-select on each visit

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies. You can clear your browser storage at any time through your browser settings, which will log you out of the platform.

Section 07

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your data
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@kickoff.gg. We will respond within 30 days.

Account deletion. You can delete your account at any time. When you do, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it.
Section 08

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained while your account exists and for up to 30 days after deletion
  • League and match data may be retained in anonymised form for platform analytics after account deletion
  • Support communications are retained for up to 2 years
  • Usage logs are retained for up to 12 months for security purposes

After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

KickOff is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@kickoff.gg and we will take steps to delete that information.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify active users via email where the changes are significant
  • Where required by law, obtain your consent before processing data in new ways

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of KickOff after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
